# The AI label

A roster of AI artists who put out albums on their own. Each artist has a name, a genre, a sound
and a voice. A language model plans each album (title, concept, running order, a brief per song)
and writes every song's words in English; ElevenLabs Music (`music_v2_5` by default) sings them.
Every genre is a public station, and the whole catalogue has a page anybody can play.

    nixamp station label sign --roster        # the roster: three artists in each of six genres
    nixamp station label                      # who is signed, their albums, this month's seconds
    nixamp station label album KEY            # an artist's next album, now
    nixamp station label config --budget 7200 --every-days 30 --songs 8 --seconds 150

The same is under **Label** on `/radio` (nixamp.com/radio, for any server you own), as MCP
(`station_label`) and over HTTP (below).

## Files

    <root>/<genre>/<artist>/<year>-<album>/001. <title>.mp3
    <root>/<genre>/<artist>/<year>-<album>/001. <title>.lyrics.txt
    <root>/<genre>/<artist>/<year>-<album>/cover.jpg
    <root>/<genre>/<artist>/<year>-<album>/album.json

`<root>` is `ai/` inside the server's library folder (e.g. `~/Downloads/done/ai`), or
`NIXAMP_LABEL_ROOT`. The genre folder is its slug (`hip-hop`, `rnb`). Each MP3 carries title,
artist, album artist, album, genre, track `n/total`, year, the words (`lyrics-eng`), the cover, and
the rights holder as publisher and copyright (`℗ 2026 Profullstack, Inc.`). Words in the tags are
published to nichedb.dev by `lyrics-publish.ts` like any owned station's.

## The people: OpenProfile pages

Each artist is cast once by the language model: one person for a solo act, three to five for a
band, all fictional. Each member gets an OpenProfile page and a reference portrait in the
artist's folder:

    <genre>/<artist>/artist.md              the band: genre, label, members, sound, themes, discography
    <genre>/<artist>/members/<slug>.md      Role, Look, Voice, Personality, Influences, bio
    <genre>/<artist>/members/<slug>.jpg     the portrait, drawn once from the Look

The pages are the source of truth once written. Edit a member's Look or Voice by hand and the
next album plan, lyrics, cover and storyboard use the edit, so the same people appear every
time. If a portrait is missing, it is drawn again. The pages are public at
`/api/v1/label/<artist>/artist.md` and `…/members/<slug>.md|.jpg`. `/label` shows each band's
members. A bought artist zip includes them.

## Genres, the daily album, videos

- **Roster:** three artists in each of Punk, Blues, Country, Jazz, Classic Rock, Metal and
  Hip-Hop; Pop and R&B as the mainstream sellers. `genreOrder` (`--genres`) decides whose first
  albums come first.
- **Parallel:** `parallel` (`--parallel 4`) songs are made at once, never the same one twice.
- **Daily autopilot:** once every enabled artist has its first albums, `albumsPerDay`
  (`--per-day`, 1 by default) new albums a day. Each is by an artist picked at random, or with
  `newArtistChance` (`--new-artists`, 0.25) by a new artist the language model invents in one of
  the owner's genres. It runs inside the nixamp daemon on its 5-minute check, so no cron is
  needed. Plans, words and songs are saved as they are made, so a crash or a restart resumes.
  Failures back off per artist, and out of credit pauses until `resume`.
- **Music videos:** each song also gets `001. <title>.mp4`, a 720p video of its cover over a
  blurred copy with the song's waveform, made by ffmpeg one at a time. They play on a free
  "<holder> AI · Music Videos" TV station and are included in bought zips. Turn them off with
  `--no-videos`.

## Schedule and budget

- A newly signed artist gets `seedAlbums` (1) at once; after that, an album
  every `everyDays` (30), staggered so the roster does not all release on the same day.
- One album is recorded at a time across the label, a song at a time, checked every 5 minutes.
  The plan and each song's words are saved before the song is composed, so a restart, a failure
  or the month's cap picks up where it stopped.
- `monthlySeconds` (3600 by default; 0 for no cap) is seconds of music a month across the label,
  separate from AI radio's. ElevenLabs Music costs about 30 credits a second (2026-10), so an
  album of 8 songs of 150 s is about 36,000 credits.
- A provider out of credit pauses the label until `nixamp station label resume`.
- Covers come from Cloudflare Workers AI (flux-1-schnell) when the language model slot is Workers
  AI (`NIXAMP_OPENAI_URL` an account's `/ai/v1`); without one an album has no cover.

## Rights

The holder (`--holder`, "Profullstack, Inc." by default) is written into every file and declared
on each genre station with the basis `owned`. That is the holder's declaration, not legal
clearance: in the US, music and words generated by AI without human authorship may not be
protected by copyright at all, whatever the provider's terms say about who owns the output.

## The store: listening is free, files are sold

The live genre stations are always free, with their breaks every 10 minutes. The songs as files
are sold on nixamp.com through CoinPay (src/music-store.ts, src/store-routes.ts):

| | |
| --- | --- |
| a song | 10¢ |
| an album | $1 |
| everything by an artist | $2 |

- **Where to buy.** The now-playing card on a station page shows Download song / Album /
  Everything by the artist for any label song, and `/label` has the same buttons. All of them
  open `nixamp.com/buy?store=&kind=&artist=&album=&n=`. `store` is the server's opaque store id:
  the server asks nixamp.com for it once (`POST /api/v1/store/servers`, signed in with its
  `nixamp login`), and only nixamp.com turns it back into the server. Until then, and in older
  links, `server=<address>` works as before. The Buy page's cover comes through nixamp.com
  (`/api/v1/store/cover`), so the page names no music server.
- **Buying.** The buyer gives an email and picks a coin (low-fee coins first), then pays on
  CoinPay. The purchase is marked paid by CoinPay's webhook, or when the buyer returns to
  `nixamp.com/purchases/<id>?t=`. The receipt email carries that link.
- **What you get.** A zip built on the fly by the server that holds the music
  (`/api/v1/label/zip?grant=`). It holds the MP3s with the cover and words inside, each song's
  `.lyrics.txt`, `cover.jpg` and `album.json`, under `<artist>/<year>-<album>/`.
- **My music.** Signed in to nixamp.com with the buying email (or having bought while signed in),
  everything is under My music on `nixamp.com/radio`, to play or download again.
- **Grants.** nixamp.com signs a grant (what was bought, from which server, valid for a day). The
  music server asks nixamp.com whether a grant is good (`/api/v1/store/grant?g=`) before it
  serves an MP3 or a zip. An MP3 without a grant is refused.

## Followers

Follow on a `/radio/artist` or `/radio/album` page follows the artist on nixamp.com
(`artist:<name>`). The button is a small nixamp.com frame (`/follow/button`) that reads
"Following" for a follower: signing in at `nixamp.com/follow` leaves a narrow cookie on `/follow`
(SameSite=None) that can only say whom you follow, because the session cookie is SameSite=Lax and
never reaches a frame on another site. Browsers that block third-party cookies show "Follow",
which opens `nixamp.com/follow`, which knows.

When an album's first song lands, and when the album is finished, the server tells nixamp.com,
signed in as its own account: `POST /api/v1/follows/notify {target, title, body, url, key}`.
nixamp.com tells the artist's followers by mail, text and web push, as each of them chose, with a
link to the album's `/radio/album` page. An artist belongs to the first account that announces
for it; each `key` (`album:<name>:songs`, `album:<name>:done`) is told once; an artist gets at
most 4 notices a day and an account 40; links must be `/radio` pages on live.streaming or
nixamp.com (`NIXAMP_NOTIFY_HOSTS`).

## Every surface

- **CLI:** `nixamp station label …` (run the label) and `nixamp music` (catalog, buy, your
  purchases, download the zips).
- **MCP:** `station_label` (run it), `music_catalog`, `music_buy`, `music_purchases`.
- **PWA:** the Label and My music sections on `/radio`, and the My music link in the player's
  header.
- **Desktop:** the same PWA, plus "AI label catalogue" and "My music" in the Help menu.
- **API:** below.

## HTTP

Public (no key):

| | |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /label` | the catalogue as a page: every artist and album with its words, and each genre's live station (free, with breaks) |
| `GET /api/v1/label` | `{holder, genres: [{genre, station, artists: [{albums: [{songs}]}]}]}` |
| `GET /api/v1/label/:artist/:album/:n.txt` | a song's words |
| `GET /api/v1/label/:artist/:album/cover.jpg` | the album's cover |

Owner (control key or owner session):

| | |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /api/v1/stations/label` | settings, this month's seconds, what it is waiting for, the roster, artists and albums |
| `POST /api/v1/stations/label` | `{holder?, monthlySeconds?, everyDays?, seedAlbums?, songsPerAlbum?, songSeconds?, enabled?, publish?, resume?}` |
| `POST /api/v1/stations/label/run` | record whatever is due |
| `POST /api/v1/stations/label/artists` | `{preset}`, `{roster: true}`, or `{name, genre, sound, voice?, bio?, themes?}` |
| `GET/PATCH/DELETE /api/v1/stations/label/artists/:key` | `DELETE ?files=1` also deletes the artist's music |
| `POST /api/v1/stations/label/artists/:key/album` | plan the next album now |

Codes: `unknown_preset`, `name_required`, `genre_required`, `sound_required`, `already_signed`
(409), `no_writer` (409), `album_in_progress` (409), `invalid_<setting>`, `not_found` (404).

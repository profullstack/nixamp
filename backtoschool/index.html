Install the web app

Keep BackToSchool on your home screen or desktop. No app store needed.

  • iPhone or iPad: open this site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: open your browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  • Desktop: look for Install in the address bar or browser menu. The option depends on your browser.

You can also keep using BackToSchool in this browser.

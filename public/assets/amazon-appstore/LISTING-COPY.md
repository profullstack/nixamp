# English (U.S.) listing copy

## Display title

NixAmp

## Short description

Play your music and live streams on Fire TV. NixAmp connects to the NixAmp server you run, browses your library, follows live channels, and turns the biggest screen in the house into a focused remote-controlled player.

## Long description

NixAmp brings your own music library and live audio streams to Fire TV in a clean, couch-friendly interface.

Connect to a NixAmp server you control, browse its library, choose what to play, and see what is live without reaching for a keyboard. NixAmp’s television mode uses larger text, clear focus states, simple paging, and remote-friendly controls designed to remain readable across the room.

You can also explore public NixAmp streams, open live channels, save favorite servers, and move between the NixAmp machines you use. Listening to public streams does not require an account. Sign in when you want your favorites, followed streams, and servers to stay connected to your NixAmp identity.

NixAmp is built for people who want direct control over where their media lives and how it reaches their screens. Your library remains on the NixAmp server you choose. The Fire TV app acts as a player and remote; it does not upload or replace your media collection.

A running NixAmp server is required to browse and play a private remote library. Availability of public live streams depends on the broadcasters currently online.

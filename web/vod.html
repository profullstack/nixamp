    Publish yours

    Anything on your nixamp server that you may share (your own work, with permission, licensed, Creative Commons or public domain) can go here, with a link of its own.

    1. In the browser: open your server in nixamp.com signed in, play the file, then Publish on live.streaming.
    2. From the server, a whole folder at once:
      nixamp library publish ~/Videos/my-films --basis owned
      One file: its id from nixamp library search WORDS. Add --dry-run to see what it picks first; nixamp library vods lists the links.

My stations

A station plays a folder or a playlist from the server picked above, round and round, for anyone with its link. New stations are private and stopped; starting one does not publish it. Tick live.streaming or backtoschool.help on a station to put it on the air there.

AI label

AI artists who release albums on their own: a language model writes each album and its English lyrics, ElevenLabs sings them, and every genre gets a public station. Files go in the library under ai/<genre>/<artist>/<year>-<album>/, each song with its .lyrics.txt.
